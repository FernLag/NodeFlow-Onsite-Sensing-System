What this site is

NodeFlow (On-site) is a free research and outreach tool from UC Agriculture and Natural Resources and UC Santa Cruz. It assembles Arduino source code from the choices you make in a form. Using it costs nothing, and there is no account to create.

The code you download

The generated .ino file is yours. You may use, change, share and redistribute it freely, including on commercial farms and in commercial work. We ask, but do not require, that you keep the attribution comment at the top so other people can find the project.

No warranty

The generator and the code it produces are provided as is, with no warranty of any kind, express or implied, including any warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose. Sensor calibration, wiring, soil variability and hardware faults all affect what the readings mean.

Irrigation decisions stay yours. Check the readings against your own knowledge of the field before you act on them. Do not treat a number on the display as a substitute for inspecting the soil. To the fullest extent the law allows, the project team and the universities are not liable for crop loss, water waste, equipment damage or any other loss arising from use of this tool or the code it produces.

Electrical and site safety

You are responsible for wiring your own hardware safely, for the batteries and power supplies you use, and for anything you install in a field. Follow the manufacturer's documentation for the Arduino board, the LCD shield, the Irrometer adapter and every sensor.

Acceptable use

  • Do not try to overload, disrupt or probe the site or the submission endpoint.
  • Do not submit automated or bulk requests. The form is rate limited and abusive traffic is blocked.
  • Do not submit other people's personal information in the form fields.
  • Do not present the tool as your own or as endorsed by the universities without written permission.

Research participation

Downloading a file records the form details described in the privacy policy, after you have read the consent form and ticked the box. Participation is voluntary; you can ask for your records to be deleted at any time.

Availability

This is a research project, not a commercial service. The site may change or stop being available. Because it runs entirely in your browser, a copy you have already loaded keeps working offline, and a file you have already downloaded keeps working regardless.

Third-party services

Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Form submissions are recorded through Google Apps Script into a Google Sheet. Both providers handle data under their own terms.

We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated terms.
